About Us

Who We Are – Our Story & Vision

Mission, vision and value

OUR

MISSION

At Tawasul Technologies, our mission is to empower every voice—spoken or unspoken. By creating deeply empathetic and culturally-grounded technologies, we ensure no individual is silenced by disability or circumstance, enabling them to be heard in their own language, dialect, and cultural identity.

OUR

VISION

We envision a world where communication is universally accessible, where no individual is left voiceless due to disability, language, or cultural barriers. Starting with Arabic-speaking communities, Tawasul Technologies is committed to building inclusive, culturally grounded solutions that empower people of all backgrounds, children, adults, and the elderly, across underserved regions of the world. Our vision is global, rooted in the belief that every person deserves a voice.

OUR

VALUE

Communication is a Right, Not a Privilege

We believe everyone has the right to express themselves — not just those with
access, privilege, or ability.

Culture-Driven Innovation

We build tools inspired by the depth of Arabic language and lived cultural realities —
not generic templates.

Diversity Within Our Team

We embody what we stand for. Our multicultural team brings perspective, passion,
and deep empathy.

Collaboration With Purpose

Every solution is built through partnership — with therapists, parents, developers, and
users.

Technology for Good

Every feature must empower, connect, or uplift. We build tech not for tech’s sake
— but for humans.

Empathy

We listen deeply — to children, families, and professionals — and design with their lived experiences at heart.

Inclusion

We celebrate linguistic and cultural diversity, ensuring our tools reflect the voices of every community.

Cultural Diversity

Our team is proudly multicultural — coming from different countries, speaking different dialects, and bringing a rich blend of perspectives.

Innovation

We believe technology should serve humanity, and we push boundaries to create meaningful, accessible solutions.

Integrity

We work with transparency, ethical responsibility, and a commitment to always put people first.

Collaboration

We build bridges between experts, parents, designers, and communities because real impact is born from collective effort.
