Sawti’s Application

Unleash your voice ... We’re listening

Built with Care. Used with Confidence

Sawti is an Arabic-language Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) app designed to give a voice to children with speech and communication difficulties. The app prioritizes accessibility across dialects, high-quality visuals and voice output, and evidence-based therapy tools
